Your payment schedule

Payments follow construction milestones, with handover on 1 March 2028. Each instalment is a share of your total contract price (villa plus add‑ons, after promotions).

How the plan is worked out

  • Rupiah first. All transactions are in IDR. USD figures are indicative only, at IDR 17.800 per USD, as in the price list of 17 November 2026.
  • Add‑ons follow the same schedule. Their price is added to the villa and paid in the same milestone shares.
  • BPHTB on freehold only. Choosing freehold adds the 5% land and building acquisition duty, worked out on the villa price and usually settled at notarial signing. Leasehold has no BPHTB.
  • Milestones, not dates. Progress payments fall due as construction reaches each stage, so only the reservation and handover have fixed dates.
  • Not included: notary fees, PT PMA set‑up costs (for the HGB option), bank transfer charges and currency movements.
  • Promotions are applied as stated on each offer and only while it runs; one villa per offer.
