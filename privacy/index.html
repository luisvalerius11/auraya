What we collect

When you ask for the investor pack

The form asks for your name, email address, WhatsApp number and, if you choose, when you are looking to buy. With it we record the page you sent it from and the date and time.

When you contact us

If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we keep your messages and the contact details that come with them.

When you browse, only if you agree

With your consent, analytics and advertising tools record how you use the site: pages viewed, how you move through the brochure and the ROI model, buttons and links clicked, the website that referred you, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (country or city, from your IP address). These tools use cookies and similar identifiers. Nothing is collected this way until you agree in the cookie banner.

Technical data

Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages see your IP address and browser details when you load them. The site's typefaces are served from our own website, not from a third party.

Why we use it

  • To send you the investor pack and follow up by email and WhatsApp, because you asked for it. We send the pack and a single follow‑up unless you ask us for more.
  • To answer your questions and arrange viewings, because you contacted us.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it (analytics), only with your consent.
  • To measure and improve our advertising on Meta and Google (marketing), only with your consent.
  • If you reserve or buy a villa, to prepare and carry out the reservation and purchase, and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and, if you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, or stop or limit how we use it;
  • send your data to you or another organisation in a usable format;
  • stop contacting you; just reply “stop” on WhatsApp or email.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what we did before. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will reply within the time the law requires. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Keeping it safe

The site is served over an encrypted connection, form submissions travel encrypted, and only the people on our team who need your details can see them. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Children

This website is for adults considering a property purchase. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
