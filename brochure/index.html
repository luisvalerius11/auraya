Introduction

Nestled in the heart of Bali's premier clifftop destination, Auraya features a private pool and an exclusive rooftop jacuzzi, with two ways to own: a 30+20 year leasehold with the option to convert to freehold, or freehold‑equivalent HGB title through your own PT PMA.

Perfectly Positioned

The Melasti region in southern Bali is a lush, premium area renowned for having the island's finest beaches. Because of its dramatic hilly terrain, ocean‑view plots are a rare find, with the most prestigious sites already occupied by world‑class luxury resorts.

Highlights
  • Situated next to the famous Savaya club
  • The stunning Melasti Beach is about 12 minutes away by scooter
  • Beach clubs within 10 minutes; Uluwatu's other beaches and the temple within about 15 to 25 minutes
