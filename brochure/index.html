Nestled in the heart of Bali's premier clifftop destination, Auraya features a private infinity pool and an exclusive rooftop jacuzzi, with two ways to own: a 30+20 year leasehold with the option to convert to freehold, or freehold‑equivalent HGB title through your own PT PMA.
