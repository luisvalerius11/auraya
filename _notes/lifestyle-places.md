## How to collect

1. Open **Google Maps** and search the place name + "Uluwatu".
2. **Rating**: right under the name, e.g. `4.6 ★★★★★ (12,734)`. Copy both numbers.
3. **Maps link** (optional): tap **Share → Copy link**.
4. **Reviews**: open the **Reviews** tab → **Sort → Most relevant** or **Highest rating**.
   Pick 2–3 that are:
   - short (1–2 sentences) and specific (sunset, view, staff, vibe)
   - recent (last 1–2 years)
   - in English, positive, with no complaints mixed in
   Copy the text **exactly**. If a review is long, paste all of it; I'll shorten it with "…" without changing any words.
5. **Photo**: don't use photos from Google Maps (they belong to whoever uploaded them). Use:
   - your own or your team's photos (best)
   - [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) (free for commercial use): note the photographer's name
   - the venue's own photo, with their OK (e.g. message Single Fin on Instagram)

   Upload photos in the chat and I'll crop them to match the other cards.

## Not recommended

- **Bingin Beach cafés**: the beachfront buildings were demolished from July 2025; don't promote the beach-café scene
  (the new clifftop venues above are unaffected).
- **The Istana**: private event villa, not open to visitors.
- **Rock Bar (AYANA)**: iconic but in Jimbaran, outside the Uluwatu area.
