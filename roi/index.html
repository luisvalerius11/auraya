What the model assumes

  • 365 rentable nights a year. Occupancy is the share of those nights that are booked.
  • Costs are a share of revenue. Management and operating expense rise and fall with rental income.
  • Prices in USD are indicative. All transactions are in rupiah; USD figures use IDR 17.800 per USD, as in the price list of 17 November 2026.
  • A full year of rentals. The figures describe a typical year once the villa is handed over (1 March 2028) and listed.
  • Not included: income tax on rental earnings, financing costs and the optional solar package.
  • No capital growth. The ROI is cash return only; any rise in the value of the villa or land would come on top.
